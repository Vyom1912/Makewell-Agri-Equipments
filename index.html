01 — About Us

A forging tradition from Gujarat, shipped to the world.

Makewell Agri Equipments operates out of Vaktapur, Himatnagar, Sabarkantha district, Gujarat — one of India's principal manufacturing hubs for hand-forged agricultural implements.

We manufacture a focused range of tools built for one purpose: to survive real work. Every shovel, axe, mattock, hoe and spade that leaves our unit is shaped, hardened and finished by people who understand what a farmer, a forestry crew or a construction gang needs from a tool at the end of a long day.

Beyond our standard catalogue, we work directly with dealers and importers who need a specific weight, handle length or finish — manufactured to their own drawing rather than off a shelf.

03 — Manufacturing Process

From raw steel to
a dealer's shelf.

Every tool follows a fixed six-stage process — ensuring the same hardness, finish and dimensional tolerance across every batch, every season.

01

Steel Selection

High-carbon billet sourced and graded before production begins.

02

Drop Forging

Closed-die press forging shapes the head under high impact.

03

Heat Treatment

Hardening and tempering for optimal strength-to-toughness ratio.

04

Grinding & Shaping

Precision grinding to final edge geometry and surface profile.

05

Finishing & Coating

Paint, powder coat or lacquer applied per order specification.

06

QC & Packing

Dimensional check, hardness test, then export-grade packing.

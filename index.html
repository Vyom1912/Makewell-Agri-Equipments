Manufacturing Unit

✅ ISO Certified Quality

Built on Decades of Craftsmanship

Makewell Agri Equipments is a family-rooted manufacturing enterprise headquartered in Vaktapur, Himatnagar, Sabarkantha, Gujarat, India — a region long recognized as a premier hub for agricultural hand tool production.

With over two decades of hands-on experience, we manufacture and export a wide range of high-carbon steel agricultural and industrial hand tools to dealers, wholesalers, and distributors across Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Europe, and beyond.

Our state-of-the-art forging and finishing facility is equipped with modern presses, heat-treatment furnaces, and quality testing equipment, ensuring every tool that leaves our factory meets international durability standards.

Himatnagar in Sabarkantha district, Gujarat, is one of India's most prominent agricultural hand tool manufacturing clusters.
